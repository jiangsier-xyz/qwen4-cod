# Visual Reference Descriptions — COD23 / MW4-style Target (v2, official HD assets)

All images are official Activision / Infinity Ward marketing or store assets
(callofduty.com blog + landing page, Steam store), except folders 01–03 which are
1080p captures from official gameplay videos (UI screens are not published as stills).
Use them as a *fidelity and grammar* target for original content, not as assets to copy.

## 0. Poster / key art (`00-poster/`)
- `keyart-alley-textless-5120x2400.jpg` — 5120×2400 textless hero art. Three operators
  advance down a narrow wet alley at night; single warm sodium lamp top-left, cold
  rim light on helmets/optics, heavy vignette, near-monochrome desaturated grade with
  olive-drab bias. Composition: center hero largest, flanks slightly behind, weapons
  at low-ready. Defines the overall color grade and contrast curve of the game.
- `keyart-price-ghost-split-profile.jpg` — 2560×1400 split-profile key art: bearded
  man in beanie (left, warm skin tones) vs skull-mask balaclava + headset (right,
  cold grey), divided by a vertical golden strip of Korean neon signage seen from
  above. Teaches dual-temperature grading (warm human / cold gear) and signage motif.
- `keyart-alley-with-logo.jpg` — 1920×1080 version of the alley art with the franchise
  logo lockup top-center; reference for logo placement/scale on menu backdrop only.

## 1. Main menu / lobby (`01-menu/`) — framework only
- `main-menu-quickplay.jpg` — full-bleed 3D lobby backdrop (operator diorama, shallow
  DOF, dark desaturated grade); slim left icon rail; logo top-left; amber clipped-corner
  primary CTA above a grouped mode-card list; right daily-challenge panel with progress
  bars and XP badges; top-right rounded social pill. Note the clipped-corner (chamfered)
  card language, thin 1px separators, uppercase condensed headings, amber accent on
  exactly one primary action.
- `lobby-matchmaking.jpg` — same shell with a matchmaking state: mode card expands into
  a playlist checklist, search status line + cancel button replace the CTA, player
  cards row appears bottom-left. Framework: state-driven card expansion, not new pages.

## 2. Settings (`02-settings/`) — framework only
- `settings-interface-hud-tab.jpg` — horizontal primary tab bar (top) + sub-tab strip;
  left scrollable grouped option rows (label + dropdown/toggle/slider, focused row
  highlighted with an amber left edge, nested "Show More"); right detail pane with
  large option name, prose description and live preview widget; persistent footer
  hint bar (button glyphs + actions).
- `settings-audio-tab.jpg` — same shell, audio group: slider rows with numeric readout
  right-aligned, toggle rows, per-group headers in small caps; preview pane shows a
  speaker/mix diagram. Note consistent row height and 8px-grid spacing.
- `settings-controller-deadzone-preview.jpg` — detail pane hosting an interactive
  preview: two stick deadzone circles with draggable handles and min/max labels;
  demonstrates "option row left, live visual proof right" pattern.

## 3. Loadout / gunsmith / HUD (`03-system/`) — framework only
- `create-a-class.jpg` — vertical slot column (primary/secondary/perk slots) of weapon
  silhouette cards with level bars and attachment pips; right panel shows selected
  loadout summary; top match-info strip. Chamfered cards, silhouette-first icons.
- `gunsmith-attachment.jpg` — attachment list left (grouped by slot, equipped marker),
  large side-profile weapon render right with fitted attachment highlighted, pros/cons
  two-column text, stat bars with green/red +/- deltas. The render is a clean studio
  side view on dark gradient — match this presentation for the loadout screen.
- `ingame-hud.jpg` — square minimap top-left + team score bars + WINNING/LOSING tag;
  top compass tape with bearing numerals and area name; killfeed top-right; bottom-right
  ammo block (mag/reserve, weapon silhouette, fire-mode icon) + equipment slots with key
  hints; right killstreak rail + circular charge meter; center hitmarker/crosshair.
  HUD elements are thin-stroke, semi-transparent black panels, white/amber text.

## 4. Scenes / environments (`04-scenes/`) — architecture, texture, lighting grammar
- `trench-explosion-jet-dusk.jpg` — frontline trench at dusk: burned conifer trunks,
  orange fireball + spark showers, grey smoke columns against pale sky, jet silhouette
  overhead. Lighting: warm explosion key vs cool ambient sky; particulate density high.
- `neon-street-squad-rear-night.jpg` — night commercial street from behind a squad:
  stacked LED billboards (magenta/cyan/white), wet asphalt specular streaks, backpack
  fabric grain in foreground. Neon emissives are the only saturated colors in frame.
- `ktown-crab-sign-day-textless.jpg` — daytime Korean eatery street: giant orange crab
  sculpture sign, hangul vertical signboards, corrugated tile awnings, painted menu
  posters, glass storefront reflections, car roof as cover. Overcast bright key, soft
  shadows; signage layering (3 depth planes) is the facade grammar to copy.
- `forest-autumn-mist-textless.jpg` — autumn conifer forest: red-leaf canopy, knee-high
  dry grass, granite boulders, volumetric mist shafts, cold blue-shadow / warm-leaf
  complementary grade. Ground cover density and tree trunk spacing define lane reads.
- `atrium-dome-godrays.jpg` — interior transit atrium: lattice glass dome, strong
  god-ray shafts through haze, silhouetted operators, polished floor reflections,
  scattered brass casings. Backlit silhouette lighting + haze = interior mood template.
- `hotel-atrium-gasmask-railing.jpg` — ruined hotel atrium: curved brass railings,
  torn pink drapes, hanging streamers, daylight blowout through curtain-wall glass,
  debris on marble floor. Contrast of luxury finish (brass/marble) vs destruction.
- `night-car-cover-korean-plate.jpg` — night street cover fight: sedan tail-lights as
  red key light, smoke catching headlight cones, wet cracked asphalt, Korean plate
  detail, squad crouched in car shadow. Low-key lighting with practical light sources.
- `canyon-waterfall-ghillie-oni.jpg` — desert canyon map: stratified sandstone walls,
  waterfall + shallow stream crossing, splash particles, hard sun with bounce fill
  from rock. Natural cover grammar: boulders, fallen tree, water lane as flank route.
- `killblock-chopper-crash-firefight.jpg` — shoot-house style block: crashed transport
  hel fuselage as central cover, burned pickup, bullet-chipped concrete pillars,
  container stacks, hard noon sun + short shadows. Destroyed-vehicle cover grammar.
- `x01-facility-courtyard-mist.jpg` — abandoned industrial facility: curved concrete
  office block, rooftop satellite dishes, red-star signage, ground fog bank, cold
  overcast grade, silhouette readability at distance. Fog-as-depth-cue template.
- `map-lineup-s1-five-maps.jpg` — official Season-1 map lineup collage (Shoot House,
  Backstab, Ministry, Overflow, Canyon) with name labels; use as *map variety* reference:
  snow village, parisian stone facade, industrial dust, desert canyon, shoot house.
- `killblock-map-art.jpg` — official Kill Block map art (header text overlay): boarded
  windows, rusted containers, hel wreckage, yellow lane markings on asphalt; study
  prop placement density and sightline blockers.

## 5. Weapons (`05-weapons/`) — modeling & material detail
- `blueprint-flatlay-four-guns-karambit.jpg` — 2560×1400 studio flat-lay of four
  blueprint weapons + karambit on black leather grain. Study: machined-aluminum
  receiver panels with engraved serials, carbon-fiber handguard weave, copper/brass
  muzzle and accent rings, red anodized parts, skeletonized stocks, shell caddies,
  optic risers, tape wraps. Part separation and material-zone contrast is the bar.
- `s1-weapon-lineup-with-names.jpg` — official side-profile renders (CSG-35 SMG,
  Cronen Osprey bullpup, JAK 12 shotgun, Talonhawk axe) on dark stone, with name labels.
  Clean orthographic-like side views: use for silhouette + attachment-slot layout
  (optic rail, muzzle, magazine, stock, grip) when modeling part meshes.
- `csg35-held-closeup-snow.jpg` — in-hand close-up of the CSG-35: top carry-handle rail,
  side charging slot, polymer lower, gloved-hand grip scale. Viewmodel-distance detail
  reference: panel lines, screw heads, selector markings must survive at this scale.
- `paris-gold-scar-and-m4-close.jpg` — two operators at ready on a Paris stone street:
  gold-finish SCAR-pattern rifle (tan receiver, bronze barrel) and black M4-pattern
  carbine with optic + magnifier. Shows finish variation (cerakote gold vs parkerized
  black) and optic/magnifier stack geometry on real weapons in scene lighting.
- `m4-optic-crouch-day-textless.jpg` — crouched soldier shouldering an M4-pattern rifle:
  carry-handle optic mount, rail handguard, magazine curve, stock tube; daylight
  material response on parkerized metal + fabric sling. Good anatomy reference for
  the rifle viewmodel and third-person hold pose.

## 6. Characters / operators (`06-characters/`) — gear layering & faces
- `price-face-snow-textless.jpg` — face close-up: skin pore/scar detail, wet beard hair
  cards, snow flakes on hair/brows, cold key + soft fill, shallow DOF background.
  Bar for head/face fidelity: layered hair, skin roughness variation, micro-scars.
- `rok-soldier-close-teal-light.jpg` — young ROK soldier in vehicle interior: digital
  camo helmet cover with mount bracket, tan plate carrier with taegeuk patch, shoulder
  patch tab, teal practical lights behind. Fabric-vs-webbing-vs-plastic material split.
- `rok-soldier-rifle-burning-street.jpg` — full upper body: helmet + goggles strap,
  headset, carrier with mag pouches, gloved hands on M4, burning street bokeh behind.
  Reference for gear silhouette layering (helmet > headset > carrier > pouches > sling).
- `gasmask-operator-scar-rain.jpg` — black-clad operator: rubber gas mask with twin
  round lenses (specular glass), hooded softshell, plate carrier, SCAR-pattern rifle
  with suppressor; rain streaks and wet fabric sheen. Mask lens material = glass over
  rubber, not flat black.
- `helo-door-squad-masks.jpg` — four operators in helicopter doorway: skull-print
  balaclava + hood + poncho, skeleton half-mask, tan helmet + mirrored sunglasses,
  black helmet + visor. Variety template for mask/headgear archetypes and squad reads.
- `hostile-alliance-four-operators.jpg` — two pairs: snow-marsh ghillie-hood + skull
  mask poncho pair vs urban plainclothes pair (female operator with badge belt,
  bearded male with SMG) on Paris cobblestones. Shows cloth-heavy vs gear-heavy
  archetypes side by side; study drape, strap routing, holster placement.
- `special-forces-four-panel.jpg` — four vertical panels: ghillie sniper with tan
  scoped rifle, rappelling gas-mask operator, jungle hooded operator wading water,
  black door-breacher with shield-line helmet. Pose + environment pairing template
  for operator select / lobby dioramas.
